## Important implementation behavior

- `PENDING` remains the only open review status.
- Review statuses remain exactly:
  `PENDING`, `APPROVED`, `REJECTED`, `REVERIFICATION_REQUESTED`, `CANCELLED`.
- OTP success and creation of the corresponding pending review round will commit atomically.
- Approval retains the reservation and moves the payment to `RELEASED`; Phase 2.9 performs settlement.
- Rejection releases the reservation and changes the payment to `CANCELLED`.
- Re-verification retains the reservation, clears the previous verification timestamp through the existing transition logic and requires a completely new OTP.
- The prior completed review remains immutable. Successful new verification creates the next numbered review round.
- Customer cancellation and officer approval will lock in the same order: transaction → review → account when necessary. Exactly one action can win.
- The losing concurrent request receives `409`, with the review-detail endpoint providing the authoritative result.
- Internal notes, OTP information, full account numbers and sensitive audit data never enter customer DTOs.

| Decision | Recommendation |
