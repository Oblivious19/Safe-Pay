SafePay is a simulated risk-adaptive pre-settlement transaction-control layer.

It gives you a chance to review and cancel eligible payments before they settle. Every payment stays within this simulated environment; no real money is moved.

  1. Choose your account and recipient, enter an amount, then confirm the payment.
  2. Low-risk payments settle immediately. Medium- and high-risk payments have a short protection window in which you can cancel.
  3. Very high-risk payments stay on hold for an administrator’s decision. They do not settle automatically.
  4. Follow the live status from your dashboard or Transactions page.
